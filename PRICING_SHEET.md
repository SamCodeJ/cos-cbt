## PAY-PER-USE (No Subscription Required)

### Pay Only for Actual Exams Conducted

**Base Pricing:**
- **Per Exam:** $50 (setup and hosting fee)
- **Per Candidate:** $0.50 (per candidate taking the exam)
- **Per Question:** $0.05 (per question in exam bank)

**Example Calculation:**
```
Scenario: 1 exam, 100 candidates, 40 questions

Exam Fee:       $50.00
Candidates:     100 × $0.50 = $50.00
Questions:      40 × $0.05 = $2.00
────────────────────────────
Total:          $102.00
```

**Volume Discounts:**
- 10-50 exams/year: **10% discount**
- 51-100 exams/year: **15% discount**
- 100+ exams/year: **20% discount**

**Monthly Cap:**
- Charges capped at equivalent subscription plan price
- Example: If usage exceeds $699, you pay max $699 (Standard Plan rate)

**Best For:**
- Seasonal testing (end-of-term only)
- Sporadic certification exams
- Pilot programs
- Organizations with <20 exams/year
