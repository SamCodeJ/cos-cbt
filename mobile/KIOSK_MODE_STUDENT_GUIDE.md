# Student Guide: Taking Exams with Kiosk Mode

## What is Kiosk Mode?

When you start an exam, the app will automatically lock your device to prevent accidentally leaving the app or getting distracted. This ensures fair testing for all students.

## What to Expect

### Before the Exam
- ✅ You can read instructions freely
- ✅ You can go back if needed
- ✅ No restrictions yet

### When You Start the Exam
- 🔒 The app will lock to prevent exiting
- 🔒 Back button will be disabled (Android)
- 🔒 Screen rotation will be locked
- 🔒 You'll see a message explaining the security mode

### During the Exam
- ✅ You can answer questions normally
- ✅ You can navigate between questions
- ✅ You can flag questions for review
- ✅ Timer continues as normal
- ❌ You cannot exit the app
- ❌ You cannot use the back button
- ❌ You cannot switch to other apps

### After You Submit
- 🔓 All restrictions are automatically removed
- ✅ You can use your device normally again

## Platform-Specific Instructions

### For Android Users

#### What Happens Automatically:
- Back button stops working (you'll see a warning if you try)
- Screen stays in portrait mode
- Status bar may be hidden
- Screen stays awake during exam

#### Optional - For Extra Security:
If your teacher requires it, you can enable **App Pinning**:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Security** (or **Lock Screen & Security**)
2. Find and enable **"App Pinning"** or **"Screen Pinning"**
3. Return to the exam app
4. Start your exam
5. Press the **Recent Apps** button (square button)
6. Tap the **Pin icon** (📌) on the exam app

**To exit after exam:**
- The app will automatically release when you submit
- If you used App Pinning: Hold **Back + Recent Apps** buttons together

### For iOS Users

#### What Happens Automatically:
- Screen stays in portrait mode

#### Required - Enable Guided Access:
iOS requires you to manually enable a security feature called **Guided Access**:

**Before or During the Exam:**
1. **Triple-click** the Home button (or Side button on iPhone X and newer)
2. Select **"Guided Access"** from the menu
3. (Optional) Draw circles around any areas you want to disable
4. Tap **"Start"** in the top-right corner

**After Submitting the Exam:**
1. **Triple-click** the Home/Side button again
2. Enter your Guided Access passcode
3. Tap **"End"** to exit Guided Access

**First Time Setup:**
If Guided Access doesn't appear:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Accessibility**
2. Scroll down to **"Guided Access"**
3. Turn it **ON**
4. Set a **passcode**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I exit the exam if there's an emergency?
**A:** Yes, but it depends on your exam settings:
- The app may log this as a violation
- Your teacher will be notified
- Talk to your teacher if you had a legitimate emergency

### Q: What if my battery is low?
**A:** 
- Charge your device before the exam
- Keep your charger connected during the exam
- The app keeps the screen on, which uses more battery

### Q: What if I accidentally close the app?
**A:**
- The app tries to prevent this with kiosk mode
- If it happens, reopen the app immediately
- Your answers are auto-saved every 30 seconds
- You may receive a violation warning

### Q: Can I use another app during the exam (calculator, dictionary)?
**A:**
- No, kiosk mode prevents switching apps
- If your teacher allows certain apps, they will provide them separately
- Using unauthorized apps may result in violations

### Q: The back button warning is annoying. How do I exit?
**A:**
- Don't use the back button during the exam
- Use the "Submit" button when you're done
- All restrictions will be removed after submission

### Q: What if the app crashes during my exam?
**A:**
- Reopen the app immediately
- Log back in
- Your exam will resume where you left off
- Answers are auto-saved every 30 seconds

### Q: My screen turned off. Did I lose my exam?
**A:**
- No, your progress is saved
- Unlock your device and reopen the app
- This may count as a violation if screen lock is enforced
- The exam will continue from where you were

## Violation Warnings

If your exam has "Screen Lock" enforcement enabled:

- **1st Violation**: Warning message shown
- **2nd Violation**: Warning message shown
- **3rd Violation**: Exam may be auto-submitted

**What counts as a violation:**
- Minimizing the app
- Switching to another app
- Phone call coming in
- Screen turning off

**Tip:** Put your phone in Do Not Disturb mode before starting!

## Tips for a Smooth Exam Experience

### Before the Exam:
1. ✅ Charge your device to at least 80%
2. ✅ Connect to stable WiFi
3. ✅ Close all other apps
4. ✅ Enable Do Not Disturb mode
5. ✅ Have your charger ready
6. ✅ Find a quiet place
7. ✅ Read the instructions carefully

### During the Exam:
1. ✅ Don't try to exit the app
2. ✅ Don't press the back button
3. ✅ Use "Next" and "Previous" to navigate
4. ✅ Use "Submit" when finished
5. ✅ Keep your device plugged in
6. ✅ Stay calm if you see the timer warning

### After the Exam:
1. ✅ Wait for submission confirmation
2. ✅ Disable Guided Access if you're on iOS
3. ✅ Check your email for results (if enabled)

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: I can't start the exam
- **Solution:** Make sure you have internet connection
- Check that you're logged in
- Verify it's the correct exam time

### Issue: The kiosk mode warning is too much
- **Solution:** This is for your protection
- Just use the in-app navigation instead of back button
- Everything will be normal after you submit

### Issue: I need to close the app urgently
**Android:**
- If App Pinning is enabled: Hold Back + Recent Apps buttons
- Otherwise: Force close in Settings > Apps

**iOS:**
- If Guided Access is on: Triple-click and enter passcode
- Otherwise: Swipe up (iPhone X+) or double-click Home button

### Issue: Screen turned off during exam
- **Solution:** Unlock device and return to app
- Your exam will continue
- Answers are saved automatically

## Contact Support

If you experience technical issues during an exam:
1. Try closing and reopening the app
2. Check your internet connection
3. Contact your teacher or exam proctor
4. Take a screenshot of any error messages

**Remember:** Kiosk Mode is designed to help you focus on your exam without distractions. All restrictions are temporary and will be removed as soon as you submit your exam.
